# AssetTrust commercial model and institutional partnership plan

**Prepared for Aturinzire Hargreave — discussion and validation, not a revenue forecast.**

## Customer groups

1. **Individual property buyers and owners:** organized document vault, case requests, cited evidence summaries, monitoring reminders and professional coordination.
2. **Law firms and licensed surveyor practices:** shared workspaces, document review tasks, case notes, audit activity and client report drafting.
3. **Property agencies and developers:** multiple concurrent due-diligence cases and reusable compliance processes.
4. **Banks and lenders:** workflow and evidence packs for internal assessment subject to their lending and land-valuation policies.
5. **Cooperatives and institutional asset owners:** machinery/asset records, ownership claims, maintenance and transfer evidence.
6. **Government and land administration partners:** only through agreements that define lawful data access, institutional roles and permitted representations.

## Potential income channels

| Offer | Illustrative pricing concept | Delivery obligations |
|---|---|---|
| Self-service vault | Free/low monthly price | Private secure records and authorized sharing |
| Guided verification request | UGX 50,000–150,000 platform fee | Case coordination and documented evidence scope only |
| Professional workspace | UGX 99,000–249,000 per month | Role permissions, team collaboration, templates, reports |
| Enterprise / bank / cooperative | Quotation | SLAs, security reviews, integrations and training |
| Portfolio monitoring | Monthly per asset/organization | User-selected reminders; official registry notifications only if authorized |
| Independent professional services | Pass-through/partner arrangement | Separate lawyer/surveyor engagement and transparent fees |

All numbers are hypotheses. Do customer discovery, operational costing, market testing and legal review before publishing prices. Official fees and third-party professional fees must never be misrepresented as platform revenue.

## Illustrative unit economics

A UGX 100,000 **platform service fee** might involve UGX 30,000 internal processing labor, UGX 10,000 computing/support allocations and UGX 5,000 fraud/compliance reserves. The resulting UGX 55,000 contribution is illustrative, before tax, sales costs, overhead and any external professional services. These costs are not measured and should not guide a real investment decision.

**Formula:** service contribution = collected platform revenue − directly attributable service costs − payment/chargeback costs − required refunds. External official fees passed through to authorities must be accounted for separately. Do not confuse issued quotes, invoices, settlements and collected revenue.

## Partnership sequence

Start with 5–10 interviews each from buyer/owner, lawyer, surveyor and property-agent segments. Validate whether firms will pay to organize their own work even without live registry integrations. Pilot with a small accredited professional network using clearly scoped manual cases and consent forms. Separately approach official institutions for permissible search methods and licensing. Only after controlled operations should the platform seek institutional banking or government deployments.

## Compliance and brand guardrails

- Never advertise “government certified” without legal authority and an actual agreement.
- Never say “blockchain guaranteed ownership” or “risk-free property purchase.”
- Disclose report scope, date, evidence relied on, limitations, reviewer's identity and jurisdiction.
- Provide a formal complaint and correction process and make disputes visible to authorized parties.
- Avoid exposing identity document numbers and ownership details to the public.
- Review professional services arrangements, taxation, data protection and land law with Ugandan counsel.

## Business milestones

**0–30 days:** prototype usability studies, 15+ customer interviews, pricing and unit-cost tests. **30–60 days:** security-hardening backlog, role/credential verification, initial partner agreements. **60–90 days:** small fictional/staged data pilot, then a properly consented live pilot after legal and security approval. **Beyond:** subscription pilots and paid case services, approved institutional integrations, new asset classes and neighboring markets only when their legal frameworks are understood.
